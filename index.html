Your LinkedIn connections as bubbles: industry, then company, then the people and titles inside.

Drop your LinkedIn export here
  1. On LinkedIn, open Settings, then Data privacy, then Get a copy of your data.
  2. Pick Connections only, request the archive, and download it from the email.
  3. Drop the zip file above, unzipped or not. Connections.csv is found even inside folders or zips within zips.
